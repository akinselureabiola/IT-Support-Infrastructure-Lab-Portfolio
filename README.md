## Troubleshooting Approach

For each scenario, I follow a structured troubleshooting process:

1. Understand the issue: I clarify the symptoms, impact, and what changed.
2. Establish scope: I determine whether the issue affects one user, multiple users, or the wider environment.
3. Check the basics: I verify connectivity, account status, services, configuration, and system health.
4. Gather evidence: I review error messages, logs, configuration, and test results.
5. Isolate the cause: I also narrow the issue down to the most likely component or configuration.
6. Apply the fix: I make the appropriate change while considering security and business impact.
7. Verify the resolution: I confirm the system works as expected and, where possible, validate with the user.
8. Document the outcome: I record the root cause, resolution, and any recommendations for preventing recurrence.
